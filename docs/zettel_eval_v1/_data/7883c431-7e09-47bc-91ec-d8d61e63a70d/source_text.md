## The Call to War and Preceding Context
- Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman (MBS) reportedly urged President Trump to attack Iran in the weeks leading up to Trump's announcement of 'major combat operations'.
- This lobbying occurred despite U.S. intelligence assessing no imminent threat from Iran.
- The push for war followed billions in Saudi investments in projects that enriched Trump and his son-in-law, Jared Kushner.
- Trump installed Kushner as a top negotiator with Iran, who, along with Middle East envoy Steve Witkoff, participated in a failed mediation session in Geneva.
- Trump had previously stated in June 2025 that more limited strikes had 'completely obliterated Iran’s nuclear capability'.

## Jared Kushner's Financial Entanglements
- Kushner's private equity fund, Affinity Partners, received a $2 billion investment in 2021 from the Saudi Public Investment Fund (PIF).
- MBS personally approved this investment, overriding a PIF screening committee's recommendation against it due to Kushner's 'inexperience' and 'excessive' fees.
- PIF pays Kushner an annual 1.25% fee ($25 million), with an estimated $137 million projected by August 2026.
- In September 2025, PIF and Affinity Partners were part of a group that acquired Electronic Arts for $55 billion, marking the largest leveraged buyout in history.
- The UAE also invested approximately $200 million in Affinity Partners, with additional funds coming via Lunate, a firm tied to UAE sovereign wealth funds.
- Kushner initially stated in February 2024 that he was just an 'investor,' but later argued in October 2025 that his financial dealings represented 'experience and trusted relationships'.

## Steve Witkoff and UAE Investments
- Steve Witkoff, Middle East envoy, is a co-founder of the crypto firm World Liberty Financial (WLF).
- Witkoff retains an 8-figure stake in WLF, a company in which the Trump family also owns a significant piece.
- Days before Trump's inauguration, the UAE's national security advisor, Sheikh Tahnoon bin Zayed Al Nahyan, purchased 49% of WLF for $250 million.
- Of this purchase, $187 million was directed to Trump family entities and $31 million to the Witkoff family.
- In May 2025, a company controlled by Tahnoon purchased $2 billion in crypto tokens from WLF.

## Donald Trump's Financial Interests
- The war began weeks after PIF financed a $7 billion deal with the Trump Organization.
- This deal involved building a Trump-branded hotel, golf course, and 500 mansions (priced $6.7M-$24M) within the $63 billion PIF-funded Diriyah development.
- Trump maintains ownership of the Trump Organization via a trust that ethics experts describe as 'meaningless'.
- Trump receives about 80% of such licensing fees from these deals.

## The War's Immediate Aftermath
- Trump announced the war in a short video on Truth Social from Mar-a-Lago, where he later attended a $1 million-per-plate fundraiser.
- By the following day, the conflict had resulted in three U.S. and hundreds of Iranian deaths.
- Iranian retaliatory strikes targeted Riyadh and locations in the UAE.
