## Chapter walkthrough

### Journey to Japan's Northern Edge
- The video begins by introducing the Kuril Islands, a territory controlled by Russia but claimed by Japan, situated just kilometers from Japan's northern edge.
- The dispute over these islands dates back to World War II, forming a significant point of contention between the two nations.
- Filmmakers embark on a journey along Japan's coast, traveling from Rausu to Shibetu and Nemuro, seeking direct experiences related to the conflict.
- The town of Nemuro is identified as a central hub for understanding the local impact and proximity to the disputed islands.
- During their travels, the filmmakers encounter local tension, including a confrontation with an elderly woman and the widespread presence of signs demanding the islands' return.

### Historical Roots of the Conflict
- Historically, the indigenous Ainu people inhabited the Kuril Islands until the 1700s, predating Russian and Japanese imperial claims.
- The 18th and 19th centuries saw increasing competition between the expanding Russian and Japanese empires for control over the territory.
- By the 19th century, Japan had incorporated the islands and implemented assimilation policies targeting the Ainu population.
- During World War II, the Kuril Islands served as a crucial Japanese military base, notably as the launch point for the 1941 Pearl Harbor attack.
- In the final days of WWII in 1945, the Soviet Union invaded the islands, leading to the displacement of an estimated 17,000-20,000 Japanese residents after Japan's surrender.

### Contemporary Realities and Human Impact
- From Nemuro, the filmmakers observe houses on the islands, speculating that these structures now primarily serve as military bases.
- At the Northern Territories Museum, they meet a historian who is also a former resident of the islands.
- The historian explains that visa-free visits, previously allowed for former residents to visit ancestral graves, were indefinitely suspended following the war in Ukraine.
- Despite his personal displacement and the ongoing geopolitical tensions, the former resident expresses no resentment towards Russians.
- He maintains a "Russian room" in his home, filled with souvenirs from past Russian visitors, symbolizing a desire for personal connection despite political divides.

## Closing remarks
- Recap: The video underscores how historical conflicts, even decades old, continue to shape geopolitical landscapes and impact individual lives, demonstrating resilience and personal diplomacy amidst international disputes. It highlights the complex interplay between national claims and the enduring human desire for connection and remembrance.
