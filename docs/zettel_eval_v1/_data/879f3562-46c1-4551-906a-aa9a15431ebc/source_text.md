## Chapter walkthrough

### Historical Context and Microdosing Definition
- Psychoactive mushrooms have a history spanning thousands of years, with Fly Agaric considered the oldest intoxicant.
- Indigenous ceremonies in Mexico have long utilized psilocybin mushrooms for their effects.
- A "heroic dose" of 5 grams of psilocybin can profoundly alter reality for several hours.
- Johns Hopkins research found that a synthesized equivalent dose significantly reduced depression and anxiety after a single session.
- Dr. James Fadiman coined the term "microdosing," defining it as taking 5-10% of a regular dose intermittently.

### Reported Benefits and Mechanisms
- Fadiman's initial data, gathered from self-reported surveys due to legal constraints, listed numerous benefits.
- Reported improvements included reduced anxiety, depression, headaches, PMS, and migraines, with some users experiencing 80-90% reduction in migraines.
- Users also reported enhanced learning, focus, work quality, and a reduction in procrastination.
- Potential mechanisms for microdosing's effects include a placebo response, systemic support like a vitamin, mitochondrial support, or increased neuroplasticity.
- Unintended benefits frequently reported were healthier eating, more exercise, and decreased use of substances like opiates, tobacco, alcohol, and caffeine.

### Expert Perspectives and Dosing Guidance
- Microdosing coaches assist individuals in identifying their optimal "sweet spot" dose, aiming for benefits without noticeable psychoactive effects.
- Dr. Fadiman critiqued a recent study that found no effect, suggesting its use of healthy individuals limited measurable improvement.
- Psychedelic psychotherapist Irene Dubin observes that her clients report improved mood and reduced chronic pain from sub-perceptual doses.
- Dubin notes that these beneficial effects typically become apparent after one to two weeks of consistent microdosing.

### Personal Experience and Resources
- The narrator's personal 25-day microdosing trial yielded several positive outcomes.
- These benefits included better organization, an improved mood, and easier completion of monotonous tasks.
- The narrator also experienced a reduction in negative self-talk during the trial period.
- A slight decrease in complex problem-solving ability was noted, comparable to the effect of consuming half a glass of alcohol.
- Due to legal status, the narrator cannot recommend microdosing but directs interested individuals to Dr. Fadiman's book and the forthcoming app at microdosing.com for data collection.
