## Counterarguments
- The argument based on total electricity consumption is flawed as it does not account for residential usage (which increased significantly due to electrification) and the lower electricity demands of a services-based economy.
- The paper's use of 'may' indicates a lack of definitive accountability.
- The paper's findings contradict those of reputed international institutions like the World Bank and IMF, which generally rely on official Indian national accounts.
- The Indian government is already adapting new methodologies, suggesting adjustments are underway or have been made (e.g., adjustment to ~4 trillion from 4.1 without including UPI).
- The authors, being 'former' officials, might have motivations to fuel propaganda or seek funding.
- Access to electricity, even for residential use, can indirectly impact GDP by enabling night shifts, commercial activity, and supporting service sector employees.
