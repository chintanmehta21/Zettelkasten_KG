## Reply Clusters
- Government Intervention and Unintended Consequences: Government attempts to support an industry can lead to unexpected and sometimes humorous outcomes, demonstrating market distortions and the challenges of central planning.
- Evolution of Property Rights in Environmental Economics: Local communities can develop effective, informal property rights systems to manage common resources, even in defiance of formal legal frameworks, illustrating solutions to the tragedy of the commons.
- Market Manipulation and Futures Trading: Historical attempts to corner commodity markets highlight the risks and regulatory responses to speculative trading and market power.
- Game Theory and the Evolution of Trust: Exploring the evolution of trust provides an interdisciplinary approach to understanding cooperation, challenging assumptions of purely rational actors in economic theory, and is highly engaging for students.

### Government Intervention and Unintended Consequences
- The 'government cheese' program, where the US government bought surplus cheese to support dairy farmers, leading to massive stockpiles of low-quality cheese and the employment of cheese tasters.

### Evolution of Property Rights in Environmental Economics
- The Maine lobster fisheries, where local communities established property rights in lobster beds to manage the resource, despite existing US law.

### Market Manipulation and Futures Trading
- The attempted cornering of the onion market, which led to the Onion Futures Act, prohibiting futures trading in onions.

### Game Theory and the Evolution of Trust
- The concept of the 'evolution of trust' as an extension of game theory, with interactive online resources available for teaching.
