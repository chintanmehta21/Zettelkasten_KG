## Investigation Scope and Findings
- Over 11.9 million documents leaked from 14 offshore service firms.
- Investigated for two years by the International Consortium of Investigative Journalists (ICIJ) and over 600 journalists from 150 outlets.
- Uncovered financial secrets of 35 current and former world leaders, over 330 politicians and public officials in 91 countries, and over 130 billionaires.
- Revealed how powerful figures benefit from an offshore system they could help end.
- An ICIJ analysis identified 956 offshore companies tied to 336 high-level officials, with over two-thirds in the British Virgin Islands (BVI).

## Scale of Offshore Holdings
- At least $11.3 trillion is held offshore, per a 2020 OECD study, though the amount tied to crime is unknown.
- The leak is larger than the 2016 Panama Papers.
- Reveals the owners of over 29,000 offshore companies from more than 200 countries.
- Largest contingents of offshore company owners are from Russia, the U.K., Argentina, and China.

## Prominent Individuals Implicated
- King Abdullah II of Jordan secretly purchased 14 luxury homes worth over $106 million through at least 36 shell companies.
- Czech Prime Minister Andrej Babis used shell companies to inject $22 million to buy a French chateau, which he did not declare.
- Former British Prime Minister Tony Blair and his wife acquired an $8.8 million London building by purchasing its BVI-based parent company, saving over $400,000 in property taxes.
- Other leaders implicated include the presidents of Ukraine, Kenya (Uhuru Kenyatta, whose family has over $30 million in offshore assets), and Ecuador (Guillermo Lasso, who moved assets to South Dakota trusts after a law forbade officials from using tax havens).
- Public figures like Sachin Tendulkar, Shakira, and Claudia Schiffer were named, with their representatives claiming their finances are legitimate.
- Italian mobster Raffaele Amato, now serving a 20-year sentence, was also exposed.

## Facilitators of the Offshore System
- The system is enabled by elite law firms, banks, and accounting practices in the U.S. and Europe.
- Baker McKenzie, the largest U.S. law firm, worked for clients like Ukrainian oligarch Ihor Kolomoisky (accused of laundering $5.5 billion) and fugitive Jho Low (accused of embezzling over $4.5 billion from 1MDB).

## The United States as a Secrecy Jurisdiction
- The U.S. itself is a major secrecy jurisdiction.
- States like South Dakota saw trust assets quadruple to $360 billion in a decade.
- The U.S. pressures other nations for transparency on American accounts but has refused to join a reciprocal agreement supported by over 100 jurisdictions.

## System Resilience and Adaptability
- The offshore system is highly adaptable.
- After the Panama Papers exposed Mossack Fonseca, hundreds of its clients simply moved to other providers.
